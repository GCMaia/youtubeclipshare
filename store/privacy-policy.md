# Clip Share for YouTube: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 17, 2026_

Clip Share does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information or browsing data.

## The browser extension

- **Where it runs:** only on www.youtube.com.
- **What it reads:** the ID of the video you are watching and its current playback time. It uses them to build clip links and to pause a clip at its end time. This happens entirely in your browser.
- **What it keeps:** start and end times you type are kept in memory only while the YouTube tab is open. The only thing saved is your choice of link type ("Link for everyone" or "YouTube link"). It is stored in your browser's extension storage, which your browser may sync to your other devices.
- **No tracking:** there are no analytics, ads or servers, and the extension makes no network requests of its own.
- **Clip links:** a link you copy contains only the YouTube video ID and the start and end times. You decide where to share it.

## The web player (links for everyone)

"Link for everyone" links open a static web page at youclip.ca. The page plays the clip with YouTube's privacy-enhanced embedded player (youtube-nocookie.com).

- The page has no analytics, ads or cookies of its own, and it doesn't store anything. The clip (video ID, start and end time) is read from the link itself.
- The video is played by YouTube, so [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies to the player.
- youclip.ca is hosted by Cloudflare, which may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and request times, to deliver and protect the site. See [Cloudflare's Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
